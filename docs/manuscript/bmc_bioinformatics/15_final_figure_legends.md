# Figure legends

## Figure 1. A frozen healthy chamber reference defines the acrf measurement framework

(A) Donor-paired chamber estimand used throughout. Rhythm is assigned at the donor level, whereas chamber is measured within donor. The raw LA−RA contrast is direction-aligned by the frozen healthy sign MATH:s_p to yield MATH:aCAI_dp, then scaled by the program-specific healthy magnitude MATH:M_p to yield MATH:aCAIrel_dp. (B) Healthy-reference scoring of the 30 frozen programs in eight paired LA/RA donors from GSE112339. Points show donor-level aligned aCAI values; diamonds mark MATH:M_p (mean absolute aligned healthy contrast). Twenty-two programs are LA-biased and eight are RA-biased; all 30 programs matched the canonical direction in every donor (30/30 programs × 8/8 donors). (C) Selection provenance for the frozen panel: 80 candidates → 38-program strict pool → 25 strict plus five fallback programs completing the 30-program object. Disease cohorts were not used for reselection, leading-edge membership, chamber direction, or family assignment. (D) Frozen mapping of 30 programs into 16 families with constituent programs, canonical chamber direction, and display order. Family summaries use equal-weight program-first aggregation; GeoMx uses a predefined family gene-union exception without altering topology. Boundary: the healthy reference freezes coordinate definitions before AF deployment and does not select disease signals.

## Figure 2. Benchmarking chamber-aware calibration across atrial cohorts

Limited, prespecified benchmark of full acrf and comparators under shared frozen programs and the same 51 paired AF/SR donors from three cohorts (GSE128188, GSE79768, GSE115574). (A) Method definitions: A1 full acrf (donor pairing, direction alignment by MATH:s_p, healthy-magnitude scaling by MATH:M_p, program-first family aggregation, Hedges’ g); A2 no donor pairing; A3 no direction alignment (native LA−RA orientation retained); A4 no healthy-magnitude scaling; A5 chamber-agnostic averaging; B1 plain singscore evaluated separately in LA and in RA. Estimands are not all equivalent. (B) Cross-cohort effect-sign concordance (M2; concordant cohort-pair signs / 48 family–pair cells). (C) Pairwise family-effect Spearman correlations (M1; median and range across the three cohort pairs). (D) Consistency-class stability relative to A1 (M5 retained classes; High/Moderate/Low counts). Metrics describe comparability and stability, not accuracy against an external biological ground truth. For A3, higher M1 can reflect retention of native orientation rather than recovery of a preferred disease mechanism. Boundary: no result-dependent filtering was performed; wall-clock runtime (M8) was not used for ranking; peak memory (M9) was not measured.

## Figure 3. Paired-chamber bulk cohorts reveal selective cross-cohort remodeling

Frozen acrf deployment across three parallel paired-chamber bulk cohorts (GSE128188, GSE79768, GSE115574; 51 paired donors; 25 AF and 26 SR) without designating any cohort as discovery or replication and without cohort-specific pathway reselection. (A) Analysis design and cohort composition under fixed program definitions and leading-edge gene sets. (B) Donor × cohort × rhythm view of family-level MATH:aCAIrel on the frozen 16-family topology. (C) Cohort-specific descriptive remodeling states (Amplified, Preserved, Attenuated, Reversed) assigned by prespecified AF-to-SR rules with an SR-mean floor of 0.02; states are descriptive summaries, not independent significance tests. Across 48 family×cohort cells the calls comprised 5 Amplified, 16 Preserved, 15 Attenuated, and 12 Reversed. (D) Cohort-level AF-minus-SR Hedges’ g with uncertainty for each family. (E) Fixed-effect inverse-variance pooled effects graded as High, Moderate, or Low consistency (six High, eight Moderate, two Low). All six High pooled AF-minus-SR effects were negative on the aligned family scale. Boundary: consistency classes summarize cross-cohort agreement and are not causal claims or multiplicity corrections.

## Figure 4. LA single-nucleus data resolve selective family localization

LA-only single-nucleus localization in GSE255612 (18 AF and 16 non-fibrillating control donors) using donor × compartment pseudobulks across nine LA compartments (atrial cardiomyocytes, fibroblasts, endothelial cells, macrophages, lymphocytes, pericytes, vascular smooth muscle cells, adipocytes, and neuronal cells). Frozen programs and program-first family aggregation were carried forward without reselection. (A) Localization design: exact-reference scoring → AF-minus-control family contrasts → carrier-relative classification against the predefined HCA LA carrier prior. (B) Family × compartment matrix of AF−control family-mean score differences; markers indicate nominal permutation support (P < 0.05). (C–D) Example donor-level contrasts illustrating Fidelity, Relay, and Extension classes. Final family classes comprised one Fidelity family (F10), two Relay families (F11 and F15), one Extension family (F16), and 12 Below-detection families. Boundary: classes use nominal permutation support and are not FDR-controlled discovery; Below-detection indicates that no included LA compartment met preset support criteria and does not imply absence of remodeling; the resource does not independently retest paired LA–RA remodeling.

## Figure 5. Cross-sectional AF-duration analysis identifies selective chronicity trends

Duration context in GSE41177 using LAA samples from 12 AF donors with finite numeric month-coded duration and frozen family activity scores rather than MATH:aCAIrel, because the resource is not a paired LA–RA duration design. (A) Analysis design: primary LAA Spearman correlations with average ranks for ties; LAPV regional sensitivity only (Same/Opposite/NA). (B) Family-level LAA and LAPV Spearman MATH:rho_LAA on a fixed −1 to +1 scale without p-value stars. (C) Chronicity classes from full-precision |MATH:rho_LAA|: Strong-trend if ≥0.4, Weak-trend if 0.2 ≤ |ρ| < 0.4, Stable if <0.2, yielding two Strong-trend, six Weak-trend, and eight Stable families. (D) Donor-level exemplars for selected families among the 12 primary AF-LAA donors. Two-sided permutation P values (B = 10,000; seed 20260418) and donor-level bootstrap 95% intervals (5,000 replicates) summarize uncertainty but do not assign class. Boundary: cross-sectional duration supplies chronicity context and does not establish temporal progression, recurrence, or treatment response.

## Figure 6. Rule-based cross-layer prioritization and ROI-level spatial context

(A) Three-gate Path A architecture placed on the frozen 16-family topology after separate evaluation of the paired-bulk, LA single-nucleus, and duration layers: High bulk cross-cohort consistency; single-nucleus class other than Below-detection; duration class other than Stable. HCA carrier labels, GeoMx, genetics, and GSE238242 sensitivity do not enter the support count. (B) Family support counts after applying the three gates; F10 and F15 satisfied all three gates (3/3). (C) Path A summary relative to partial-support families (one 2/3, ten 1/3, and three 0/3). (D) GeoMx ROI-level spatial context from GSE261363: 24 eligible cardiac atrial-appendage DNA-positive ROIs from four patients (full resource 48 ROIs). Prespecified ROI-level summaries include Spearman correlations for F10 versus a fibroblast panel and F15 versus a macrophage panel, and a Welch AF-versus-SR contrast for F9. Boundary: Path A is rule-based triage across non-exchangeable layers, not a joint statistical test or multiplicity-controlled discovery criterion; GeoMx provides descriptive spatial context only and did not modify Path A classification.

## Figure 7. Genetic calibration reveals limited, non-nested traceability

(A) Anchor-level calibration for 12 anchors (PRELP, OMD, IDH2, NDUFA8, BMP10, SMAD9, SMAD6, NTRK2, NTF3, and FRS2, plus PITX2 and KCNN3 controls) against local AF GWAS support (±1 Mb; conventional SCI:5e-8 threshold), Bonferroni atrial eGene status (α = 0.05 / MATH:n_cis), and coloc.abf same-variant support (MATH:PP4 ≥ 0.70; priors MATH:p1 = MATH:p2 = SCI:1e-4, MATH:p12 = SCI:1e-5). (B) MATH:PP3/MATH:PP4 configuration and lead AF GWAS–eQTL separation; all 12 anchors were PP3-dominant and none met MATH:PP4 ≥ 0.70. (C) Family-level overlap with four curated external AF gene sets derived from Yuan, Roselli, Selewa, and Choi against the 14,635-gene frozen acrf-eligible background (64 one-sided hypergeometric tests with Benjamini–Hochberg correction). (D) Non-nested traceability profiles summarizing local GWAS, eGene, colocalization, and external membership flags without ordinal scoring. Observed core flags: local GWAS 7/12, eGene 1/12, same-variant colocalization 0/12. Boundary: genetics did not enter Path A; open symbols indicate evaluated absence of support and do not exclude biological involvement or convert genetics into an exclusion criterion.
