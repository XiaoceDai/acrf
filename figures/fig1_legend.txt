Figure 1. Healthy LA/RA chamber programs and their baseline cellular carriers.

Color convention (all Figure 1 panels):
  - LA = red (#D6604D)
  - RA = blue (#4393C3)

(a) PCA (healthy paired bulk RNA-seq; GSE112339).
    Each point is one sample (LA or RA); paired chambers from the same donor
    are connected by a thin line. This panel visualizes global donor-level
    and chamber-level transcriptomic structure.

(b1) Volcano (paired LA vs RA differential expression).
    x-axis: logFC (LA - RA); y-axis: -log10(FDR). Significant genes
    (FDR < 0.05) are colored by direction (LA-high red, RA-high blue),
    and representative genes are labeled.

(b2) Heatmap (top paired DE genes).
    Top chamber-biased genes (selected from paired DE statistics) are shown
    as row-z-scored voom logCPM across donor-paired samples. The top strip
    marks chamber identity for each column.

(c) Dual-method pathway selection (curated primitive).
    Candidate pathways are plotted by signed GSEA NES (x) and GSVA paired
    logFC (y). Point shade indicates leave-one-donor-out stability count
    (0/8 to 8/8). Highlighted points are the final NCP_v2 pathways selected
    by dual-method consistency, high stability, and Jaccard redundancy control;
    the selected pathway list is shown in the right subpanel.

(d) Donor-level aCAI primitive at healthy baseline.
    For each NCP_v2 program and donor, aCAI = mean log2(LA) - mean log2(RA).
    Heatmap cells show per-donor aCAI; adjacent bars show mean aCAI across
    donors; n/8 indicates sign concordance across donors (green 8/8 means
    fully direction-consistent).

(e) Baseline axis families from cross-donor aCAI patterns.
    Programs are clustered using distance = 1 - Pearson correlation with
    average linkage, with LA- and RA-biased programs clustered separately.
    Tree cut at h = 0.35 defines axis families, summarized by family ID,
    representative pathway, and number of programs.

(f) Cell-compartment attribution at healthy baseline (GSE255612 Ctrl donors).
    For each NCP_v2 program, member-gene mean expression is computed per
    compartment and row-z-scored across reliable compartments.
    Black boxes mark the dominant carrier (max row z-score) per program, and
    the right column reports the dominant compartment plus z-score.
    Compartments with n_ctrl < 5 are shown as N/A (grey) and excluded from
    row-z computation.
